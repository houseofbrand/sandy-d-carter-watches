# SANDY D CARTER dial-branding standard

This standard applies to every watch dial shown in website photography, concept art, campaign visuals, collection previews and future product imagery.

## Mandatory dial lock-up

- Place the official **SC** monogram in the upper dial area, centred below 12 o’clock.
- Place the exact wordmark **SANDY D CARTER** directly below the monogram.
- Place **AMSTERDAM** in the lower dial area, centred above 6 o’clock.
- Preserve the supplied logo geometry, letterforms, spacing and proportions. Never redraw, stretch, condense or substitute the identity.
- Use black, white or a restrained metallic treatment only when contrast is sufficient and the result remains crisp at the intended display size.

## Composition rules

- Keep the lock-up aligned to the dial’s vertical axis.
- Scale the identity to remain subordinate to time-reading information and proportionate to the available negative space.
- Do not overlap hands, indices, complications, minute tracks or the dial edge.
- Preserve the original watch design, dial colour, case, strap, lighting and composition unless a separate approved brief requests a change.

## Asset and QA rules

- Retain original images; publish edited versions as separate versioned assets.
- Optimise exported web images without visible loss of detail.
- Review every visible dial at full resolution and at its actual website display size.
- Confirm the monogram, full wordmark and AMSTERDAM line are present wherever the dial is large enough for the text to remain legible.
- Reject images with distorted lettering, invented marks, misplaced text, poor contrast or accidental changes to the watch.
