About SANDY D. CARTER

A BRAND BUILT
AROUND DESIGN.

A watch-design journey that began in Amsterdam in 2011.

TIME REFINED

SANDY D. CARTER carries forward a shared watch-design perspective that began with Sandy and Carter in Amsterdam in 2011.

The brand was founded by Rajan Mehta, who met Sandy and Carter personally during his time in Amsterdam. Their appreciation for thoughtful detailing, minimalist aesthetics and purposeful design became part of the journey behind bringing the identity forward.

Our design lens

Amsterdam’s architecture and Dutch culture of functional creativity shape the visual language: clear structure, purposeful simplicity and character through proportion.
