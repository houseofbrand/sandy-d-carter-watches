One shared perspective

Together, Sandy and Carter form the creative identity behind a Netherlands-developed premium lifestyle watch brand. They are creative personalities, not people with invented biographies, qualifications or studio locations.

The meaning of D

The letter D pays tribute to Droog Amsterdam as a source of design inspiration. No official commercial partnership or affiliation with Droog is claimed.
