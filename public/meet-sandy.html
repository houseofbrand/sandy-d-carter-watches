Meet Sandy.

The imaginative, expressive side of the SANDY D CARTER design philosophy.

TIME REFINED

Elegance as a point of view.

In the fictional Amsterdam story, Sandy is a Dutch creative visionary with an instinct for proportion, colour and the relationship between a timepiece and personal style.

She asks how a dial feels at first glance, how a metal tone changes with light, and how restraint can make an object more memorable.
