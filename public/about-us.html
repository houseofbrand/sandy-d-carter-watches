The SANDY D. CARTER story

An idea born
in Amsterdam.A brand created for the world.

Between 2009 and 2014, founder Rajan Mehta’s years in Amsterdam became the setting for a watch-brand idea shaped by European design, creative exchange and the belief that refinement begins with restraint.

01Amsterdam · 2009–2014

The story begins with a place, an experience and an ambition—not with a legal entity in the Netherlands.

The Amsterdam beginning.

Rajan Mehta lived and worked in Amsterdam from 2009 to 2014. Immersed in the city’s design culture, architecture and instinct for purposeful simplicity, he began imagining a distinctive watch brand with a European point of view and an international identity.

The intention was never to create another conventional fashion-watch label. It was to build something more considered: watches guided by timeless aesthetics, thoughtful proportions, functional elegance and individual character.

Brand integrity: Amsterdam is the creative birthplace of SANDY D. CARTER. The brand was subsequently formalised through an Indian trademark filing; no Dutch legal incorporation or manufacturing claim is made.
02The creative collaboration

Two designers.
One shared philosophy.

During this journey, Rajan connected with two designers: Sandy, a woman, and Carter, a man. Their discussions explored how European design sensibilities could shape a contemporary watch—simple without becoming anonymous, balanced without feeling rigid, and individual without excess.

04India · December 2014

From creative concept
to commercial foundation.

The Amsterdam concept was subsequently formalised in India. The SANDY D. CARTER trademark application was filed on 17 December 2014 in Class 14, marking an important step in turning the creative vision into a commercial brand.

House Of Brands Fashion LLP was established in India in 2015. Founded by Rajan Mehta, the business brings experience across e-commerce, product sourcing and development, brand building, retail and marketplace distribution.

05The next chapter

From Amsterdam
to the world.

Conceived in Amsterdam and established in India, SANDY D. CARTER is preparing for its renewed India launch in December 2026. The Netherlands remains the brand’s creative birthplace and an intended future market.

The wider ambition includes planned expansion into the United Arab Emirates and the United Kingdom, alongside a future office presence in Los Angeles. These are future intentions, not claims of current offices, shops or market operations.
