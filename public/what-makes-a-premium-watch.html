Look beyond the label

A useful way to judge a watch is to examine the things you can see, feel and verify.

Examine the details

Look for clear printing, aligned markers, a secure clasp and a comfortable strap. Finishing should be consistent across visible surfaces. A brand description cannot replace looking closely at the watch.

Ask for specifics

Materials, dimensions, movement details and service terms make comparison easier. Keep the exact model reference when asking questions, as specifications can vary within a collection.
