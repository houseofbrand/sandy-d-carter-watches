TIME REFINED

A signature collection represents more than a category. It is the point at which a brand’s strongest principles become one recognisable visual language.

Creativity meets precision

Sandy and Carter are the two real designer personalities behind the brand story. Their shared perspective brings creative expression and design discipline together in a philosophy shaped by simplicity, functionality and thoughtful detail.

The architecture of a watch

A dial organises information. The case frames and protects it. Hands and markers establish hierarchy, while negative space gives the eye clarity. These relationships describe a design approach; they do not announce the specifications of an unreleased model.

The signature reveal

The signature collection is planned to be unveiled as part of the SANDY D. CARTER India launch in November 2026. Final product photography and verified information will be published closer to the reveal.
