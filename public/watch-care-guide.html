Journal · Care guide

How to Care
for Your Watch

Simple habits help protect appearance and function.

TIME REFINED

Good watch care begins with accurate model information and a few consistent habits. Materials, seals, movements and straps differ, so general guidance should always be read alongside the manufacturer’s instructions for the exact watch.

Follow the model-specific instructions

Water resistance is a measured rating, not a visual quality. Never assume that a watch can be immersed because it looks robust. Ratings can also be affected by ageing seals, impact or an incorrectly secured crown. Have water resistance checked by an appropriate service professional when the manufacturer recommends it.

Clean gently

For routine dust and fingerprints, use a clean, dry, soft cloth. Do not apply perfume, solvents, detergents or abrasive cleaners. More intensive cleaning depends on the case, crystal, strap and water-resistance guidance; if those facts are not known, avoid moisture and seek qualified advice.

Leather straps should generally be kept away from water, high humidity and prolonged perspiration. Metal bracelets may tolerate different cleaning methods, but only when the watch head and its seals are suitable.

Protect the watch in daily use

  • Avoid direct impact, vibration and sudden temperature extremes.
  • Apply fragrance or cosmetics before putting on the watch and allow them to dry.
  • Keep magnetic devices away when the movement guidance warns of magnetic sensitivity.
  • Do not operate pushers or adjust the crown in conditions the manual does not permit.

Store it with care

Store the watch dry, out of direct sunlight and away from hard objects that could scratch it. A soft-lined case or separate compartment prevents contact with jewellery and metal accessories. If a watch will not be worn for a long period, follow its movement-specific storage guidance.

Use qualified service support

Battery replacement, pressure testing, movement service and case opening should be handled by a qualified provider. Service intervals vary by movement and manufacturer. SANDY D. CARTER will publish model-specific care and warranty information only when verified products are released.
