Journal · Care guide

A little care goes a long way

Simple habits help keep a watch comfortable to wear and its surfaces looking their best.

TIME REFINED

Clean gently

Use a soft, dry cloth to remove everyday marks. Avoid solvents, perfume and abrasive cleaners. For deeper cleaning, follow the manufacturer’s instructions for the case and strap materials.

Treat water ratings carefully

A water-resistance marking is not a permanent guarantee. Seals age and can be affected by damage or servicing. Keep leather straps dry and ask a qualified service professional about testing after repairs.

Store with care

Keep the watch away from hard objects that could scratch it. Allow a damp strap to air dry at room temperature, away from direct heat. Use the care instructions supplied with your model.
