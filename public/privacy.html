PRIVACY POLICY.

A clear explanation of this pre-launch website’s current data practices.

TIME REFINED

Last updated 11 November 2026

This pre-launch website currently provides information only. It does not submit or store newsletter entries, contact-form messages, payment details or customer accounts.

Information the website may receive

Standard hosting infrastructure may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested page, timestamp and referring page for security, reliability and aggregate traffic measurement. We do not use this information to create invented customer profiles.

Newsletter and contact forms

Subscription and contact collection are not active. A visible form will not pretend to submit data. If these services are introduced, this policy will identify the provider, purpose, lawful basis where applicable, fields collected, retention approach and method for withdrawing consent.

Cookies and analytics

The current site does not intentionally set advertising cookies. Hosting providers may use essential technical mechanisms to deliver and protect the service. Any future analytics or marketing technology will be documented before deployment, with consent controls where required.

Data retention and sharing

Because the website does not currently accept form submissions, it does not maintain a marketing or enquiry database. Technical hosting logs may be retained by service providers according to their security and operational policies. We do not state an unverified fixed retention period.

Your choices

You may use the site without creating an account. When a verified privacy contact channel is published, visitors will be able to ask about access, correction or deletion of information associated with them. Rights vary by location and will be handled according to applicable law.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before newsletter, account, analytics or commerce functionality is activated. The updated date above will change whenever material practices change.
