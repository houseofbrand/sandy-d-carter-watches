DESIGN IDENTITY

More than an accessory

A designer watch should express a clear visual idea. For SANDY D CARTER, that idea begins with simplicity: balanced cases, legible dials and details chosen for purpose rather than decoration. The result is a premium designer watch collection intended to feel current without becoming temporary.

Our approach was shaped through years of international watch-industry experience and creative conversations with Amsterdam-inspired designers Sandy and Carter. It combines European design sensibility with an understanding of how people in India choose and wear contemporary fashion watches.

Designed for men and women

The collection direction includes designer watches for men and designer watches for women. Each line follows the same philosophy while allowing proportion, colour and character to evolve. Final models and specifications will be published only after approval.
