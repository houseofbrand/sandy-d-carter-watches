Frequently asked questions

BRAND FACTS,
WITH CLARITY.

Factual answers about the brand, its inspiration and planned India debut.

TIME REFINED

What is SANDY D. CARTER?

SANDY D. CARTER is an emerging accessible premium lifestyle watch brand inspired by Amsterdam and European design aesthetics.

Who develops and markets the brand?

House Of Brands Fashion LLP, India.

Who are Sandy and Carter?

They are fictional creative personalities representing creative imagination and design precision. They are not founders, employees or real-world designers.

Is SANDY D. CARTER a Dutch company?

The brand is developed and marketed in India. Amsterdam and Dutch design aesthetics are sources of creative inspiration.

What watches are planned?

Contemporary quartz analogue watches for men and women, including leather-strap, stainless-steel bracelet and dress-watch directions.

When is the planned India debut?

December 2026.

What is the intended price positioning?

Approximately ₹4,000–₹10,000. Final model prices will be confirmed when approved products are revealed.
