Questions & answers

A few useful details.

When does the collection launch?+

The India debut is planned for December 2026, in partnership with Myntra. Product listings and prices will be shared at launch.

Can I buy a watch now?+

The site currently introduces the collection. Online purchasing is not yet available. Contact us with collection or availability questions.

What inspires the design?+

Amsterdam is the starting point, with Scandinavian-inspired simplicity informing proportion, colour and detail.

Who can I contact?+

Email rajan@houseofbrands.in or use the WhatsApp link on our contact page. House Of Brands Fashion LLP handles brand and partnership enquiries.

Where can I find product specifications?+

Confirmed case sizes, materials, movement details and water-resistance ratings will accompany individual product listings at launch.

What are the delivery, returns and warranty terms?+

These will be provided with the launch listings. Review the terms on the platform where you place your order.
