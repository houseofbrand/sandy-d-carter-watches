Frequently asked questions

BRAND FACTS,
WITH CLARITY.

Approved information about the brand, partnerships and planned launch.

TIME REFINED

What is SANDY D. CARTER?

SANDY D. CARTER is an emerging premium lifestyle watch brand developed in the Netherlands and inspired by Amsterdam and European design aesthetics. The brand combines Scandinavian-inspired minimalism, considered detailing and contemporary quartz timepieces for men and women.

Where is SANDY D. CARTER developed?

SANDY D. CARTER is developed in the Netherlands, drawing inspiration from Amsterdam’s design culture and European aesthetics. The brand is being developed for international markets in alliance with House Of Brands Fashion LLP, India.

Who develops and markets SANDY D. CARTER globally?

SANDY D. CARTER is developed in the Netherlands in alliance with House Of Brands Fashion LLP, India, which serves as its global development and marketing partner. The alliance focuses on brand building, collection development, market expansion and international distribution.

What is the relationship between SANDY D. CARTER and House Of Brands Fashion LLP?

House Of Brands Fashion LLP, India, is the global development and marketing partner for SANDY D. CARTER. The alliance is focused on developing the brand’s commercial presence, building its collections and establishing distribution across international markets.

Who are Sandy and Carter?

Sandy and Carter represent two complementary creative personalities that form the foundation of the brand’s identity. They symbolise creative imagination and design precision, united by a shared appreciation for considered design, clean proportions and everyday functionality.

Is SANDY D. CARTER a Dutch brand?

Yes. SANDY D. CARTER is developed in the Netherlands, with Amsterdam and Dutch design aesthetics forming an important part of its creative identity. The brand’s design philosophy reflects European sensibilities and Scandinavian-inspired minimalism.

What is the connection between SANDY D. CARTER and Droog Amsterdam?

The letter D in SANDY D. CARTER pays tribute to Droog Amsterdam, whose distinctive approach to contemporary design has influenced the brand’s creative philosophy. Droog Amsterdam is located at Staalstraat 7B, 1011 JJ Amsterdam, The Netherlands. This identifies Droog’s location and is referenced in connection with the brand’s design inspiration. It is not the brand’s registered office or operating headquarters, and no official commercial partnership or affiliation with Droog is claimed.

What watches will the brand offer?

The planned collection will include contemporary quartz analogue watches for men and women, with minimalist stainless-steel designs, leather-strap styles, stainless-steel bracelet watches and refined dress watches. The design direction emphasises clean dial layouts, balanced proportions, Scandinavian-inspired styling and understated detailing.

When will SANDY D. CARTER launch in India?

SANDY D. CARTER is preparing for its India debut in December 2026. The upcoming collection is planned to be introduced through Myntra.

Has SANDY D. CARTER partnered with Myntra?

Yes. SANDY D. CARTER has signed a partnership with Myntra for its planned introduction to the Indian market. The India debut is scheduled for December 2026.

What is the brand’s price positioning?

SANDY D. CARTER is positioned in the accessible premium segment, with an indicative price range of approximately ₹4,000–₹10,000. Final prices will be announced when individual products are officially revealed.

Will SANDY D. CARTER be available internationally?

Yes. The brand’s global development and marketing alliance with House Of Brands Fashion LLP, India, is intended to support its international expansion and distribution. Specific market launches and availability will be communicated as plans are finalised.

SANDY D. CARTER is an emerging premium lifestyle watch brand developed in the Netherlands and inspired by Amsterdam and European design aesthetics. The brand brings together Scandinavian-inspired minimalism, considered detailing and contemporary quartz timepieces for men and women.
