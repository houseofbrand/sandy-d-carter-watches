What is SANDY D. CARTER?

A watch brand carrying forward the shared design perspective of Sandy and Carter, whose watch-design journey began in Amsterdam in 2011.

Are Sandy and Carter real people?

Yes. They are the real designer personalities behind the approved SANDY D. CARTER design story. Founder Rajan Mehta met them personally during his time in Amsterdam.

What does the letter D represent?

It pays tribute to Droog Amsterdam as a design-inspiration reference. No official affiliation, ownership or business location is claimed.

Where are the watches made?

Verified origin information will be published for each approved model.

Can I buy a watch now?

No. This is a pre-launch preview and checkout is not enabled.
