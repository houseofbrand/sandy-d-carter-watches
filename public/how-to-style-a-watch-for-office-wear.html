Journal · Style guide

From work to the weekend

A watch does not need a separate wardrobe. Start with what you already wear.

TIME REFINED

Follow one detail

Take a cue from a belt buckle, a ring or a favourite pair of shoes. Repeating one tone can make an outfit feel considered without matching every accessory.

Make space at the cuff

Try the watch with your usual shirt or jacket. The case should move beneath the cuff comfortably. On relaxed days, a rolled sleeve gives the same watch a different presence.

Keep the balance personal

A clean dial is easy to wear alongside patterned clothing. With a plain outfit, colour or texture can do more of the work. Choose the combination you feel comfortable wearing repeatedly.
