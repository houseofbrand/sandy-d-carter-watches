Style guide

Metal or leather
watch straps?

How material changes a watch’s comfort, character and relationship with your wardrobe.

MATERIAL STYLE

Metal bracelets: structured and versatile

A metal bracelet can make a watch feel integrated and architectural. Link shape, taper, articulation and clasp design influence both comfort and appearance. Brushed surfaces generally feel quieter, while polished details add contrast.

Metal can work across professional and casual settings, and it is usually less affected by moisture than natural leather. Weight and fit matter: a bracelet should allow adjustment without feeling loose or restrictive.

Leather straps: warm and personal

Leather introduces texture, colour and softness. It can make the same case feel more formal, relaxed or vintage depending on finish and stitching. Over time, natural leather develops signs of wear that many people value as personal character.

Leather requires more care around water, humidity and perspiration. A well-fitted strap should sit comfortably without needing to be overtightened.

Choose for the way you live

Think about climate, clothing and how often the watch will be worn. Metal may suit an all-purpose daily watch; leather can bring warmth to a minimalist dial or create an easy wardrobe change. Interchangeable straps can extend versatility when lug width and attachment are compatible.
