Leather or metal?

Two ways to change the feel of a watch, from texture and weight to how it wears through the day.

Leather: a softer contrast

A leather strap introduces texture and gradually develops signs of wear. Keep it away from prolonged moisture and let it dry naturally if it becomes damp. A second strap can help you give each one time to air.

Metal: a more continuous line

A bracelet links the case and strap visually. Correct sizing matters: an overly loose bracelet can allow the watch to slide, while a tight one becomes uncomfortable as your wrist changes through the day.

Check compatibility first

Before changing a strap, confirm the lug width and attachment type. Some cases require a specific fit. A watchmaker can help you choose and fit a suitable replacement.
