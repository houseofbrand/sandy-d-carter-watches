Founder · Rajan Mehta

A global journey.One considered vision.

Rajan Mehta’s experience across India, Europe, the Netherlands, the Middle East and China shaped an ambition: to create a watch brand with European design roots and international relevance.

01The founder

An entrepreneur, product developer and brand builder with more than 25 years of experience in watches, fashion accessories and lifestyle products.

From product to brand.

Rajan Mehta’s career spans product development, sourcing, manufacturing, packaging, retail strategy and e-commerce. His international experience includes work across India, Europe, the Middle East and China, bringing together an understanding of how ideas move from first concept to commercial reality.

From 2009 to 2013, Rajan lived and worked in Amsterdam. The city’s creative culture and approach to purposeful design became the backdrop for the original SANDY D. CARTER idea.

The Amsterdam vision

Rajan wanted to create an international watch brand that felt considered rather than conventional. Through discussions with designers Sandy and Carter at Droog Amsterdam, that ambition developed into a philosophy centred on simplicity, proportion, balance and character.

“During my years in Amsterdam, I began imagining a watch brand that would reflect the design values I admired in Europe. SANDY D. CARTER grew from those conversations, creative perspectives and a belief that simplicity can create something truly distinctive.”Proposed founder statement · requires Rajan Mehta’s approval before it is treated as a verbatim quotation

Building the business foundation

Rajan founded House Of Brands Fashion LLP, established in India in 2015. Its experience in product development, international sourcing, e-commerce, brand building and marketplace distribution provides the commercial foundation for SANDY D. CARTER’s next chapter.

A long-term international view

His ambition is to reconnect the brand with the Netherlands, launch its renewed identity in India in December 2026, and build future relevance across the UAE, London and Los Angeles—one market at a time, without losing the clarity of the original Amsterdam idea.
